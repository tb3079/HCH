HCH is a continuous health operating system. It keeps one living model of your biology and of the life you actually lead, and it measures which changes make you healthier.

  • Biology: labs, imaging, physiology
  • Life: food, training, sleep, medication

        Stress, in the right dose, makes a cell stronger.

        That is hormesis, and it is where our name comes from. Exercise, heat, cold and metabolic load all work this way: a measured stress, then recovery, then adaptation. Too little changes nothing. Too much does damage.

        Your record goes where you go.

        A new physician shows a code. You scan it, see exactly what is being asked for and for how long, and approve. The records arrive in seconds, each with proof of where it came from.

        Built on FHIR R4, SMART on FHIR, OAuth2, HL7 v2 and C-CDA. Records stay encrypted off-chain. A ledger holds only hashes, timestamps and consent events.
